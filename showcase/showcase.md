# Follow your hunger.

Coil is a neon grid serpent about finding a rhythm in a shrinking space. Turn through a dark, enclosed arena, chase the amber pellets, and keep your bright little head out of trouble.

Every bite makes you longer. Your body remembers every turn, so the route you take becomes the next obstacle you have to solve. There is no brake, no reverse, and no second chance against the wall.

Survival is only half the pursuit. Find another pellet within three and a half seconds and your combo climbs, all the way to five times the points. The draining bar asks a simple question: take the safe route, or keep the chain alive?

Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, pause whenever you need a breath, and come back for one more run. A crisp electronic pulse, luminous scales, and a burst of amber mark each little victory.
